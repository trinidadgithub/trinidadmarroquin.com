Practical Data Science For DevOps And SRE is a hands-on series about using simple, explainable analysis methods in operational engineering work.

The goal is not prediction hype, black-box machine learning, or academic statistics. The goal is better awareness: seeing reliability patterns earlier, asking better questions during reviews, and making operational decisions with evidence instead of instinct alone.
