I work on infrastructure that needs to be understandable, repeatable, and boring in production.

My focus areas include Kubernetes platform operations, infrastructure as code, image pipelines, CI/CD, and observability. I prefer small, maintainable systems over clever ones and write here about the parts of operations that benefit from clear notes.

## What You Will Find Here

- Notes on Kubernetes, Rancher, Terraform, Packer, Concourse, and platform operations.
- Project writeups that explain tradeoffs, failure modes, and operational outcomes.
- Practical posts about reliability, deployment hygiene, monitoring, and incident learning.
- A place to keep my resume and professional background current.

## Current Interests

- Making infrastructure changes safer with reviewable plans and repeatable builds.
- Improving cluster operations without adding unnecessary control-plane complexity.
- Turning observability data into useful operational signals instead of dashboards no one reads.
- Documenting systems so the next operator has context before there is an outage.
