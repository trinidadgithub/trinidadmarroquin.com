I am Trinidad Marroquin, an SRE / DevOps engineer focused on reliable infrastructure and practical automation.

I work across the parts of the stack where build systems, deployment pipelines, clusters, and production operations meet. My day-to-day interests include Kubernetes, Terraform, Packer, Rancher, Concourse, observability, Linux systems, and the process work needed to keep infrastructure understandable over time.

I value systems that are simple enough to debug under pressure. That usually means clear ownership, reproducible builds, versioned infrastructure, useful alerts, and runbooks that explain the actual operating model.

## Engineering Approach

- Prefer explicit configuration and documented tradeoffs.
- Automate repetitive work, but keep the automation inspectable.
- Treat reliability as a product of design, operations, and feedback loops.
- Build dashboards and alerts around decisions operators need to make.
- Reduce surprise in production through small changes, clear rollbacks, and tested recovery paths.

## Areas I Work In

- Kubernetes cluster operations and platform reliability.
- Infrastructure provisioning with Terraform.
- Machine and image build workflows with Packer.
- Rancher-based Kubernetes management.
- CI/CD pipelines with Concourse.
- Metrics, logs, traces, alerting, and incident follow-up.

This site is where I collect project notes, operational lessons, and technical writing that may be useful to other engineers working on similar systems.
